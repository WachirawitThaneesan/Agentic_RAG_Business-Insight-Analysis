# Business Agent logo: usage guide

**The mark.** A lowercase **b** with a gold superscript: every answer is footnoted.
The b is Business; the superscript is the citation that every figure in the app
carries back to its PDF page. The gold square is the one detail to protect.

## Files

| Need | File |
|---|---|
| App header, docs, slides (light ground) | `svg/lockup-horizontal-color.svg` |
| Dark ground (the app's default theme) | `svg/lockup-horizontal-reversed.svg` |
| Square spaces, title slides | `svg/lockup-stacked-*.svg` |
| Symbol only, 33 px and up | `svg/symbol-color.svg`, `svg/symbol-reversed.svg` |
| Symbol only, 16 to 32 px | `svg/symbol-small-*.svg` (heavier stem, larger superscript) |
| Favicon, app icon, PWA icons | `web/` (favicon.ico, favicon.svg, apple-touch-icon.png, icon-192/512, maskable-512, site.webmanifest) |
| One colour (print, engraving, fax) | `svg/*-black.svg`, `svg/*-white.svg` |
| Name alone | `svg/wordmark-*.svg` |
| Presentation with mockups | `board/presentation.html`, `board/slides/*.png` |

The wordmark is Geist SemiBold (SIL Open Font License) converted to outlines,
tracked -0.02 em. Do not retype it; use the files.

## Colour

| Role | HEX | RGB | CMYK (approx.) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ink: the b and the name on light grounds | `#16171B` | 22 23 27 | 19 15 0 89 |
| Gold: the superscript, always | `#F2B544` | 242 181 68 | 0 25 72 5 |
| Paper: the b and the name on dark grounds | `#F2F1EE` | 242 241 238 | 0 0 2 5 |

CMYK values are conversions, not press-proofed. Match Pantone against a bridge
guide before print.

When only one colour is available, the superscript takes the same colour as the
b (black or white files). Never recolour the b gold or the superscript any other
hue.

## Clear space and size

- **Clear space:** keep at least the superscript's width (one stem width, 40
  units on the 256 grid, about 22 % of the symbol height) empty on every side.
- **Minimum size:** symbol 16 px tall on screen (use the small cut up to 32 px),
  horizontal lockup 120 px wide, 25 mm in print.
- **Lockup proportions are fixed:** in the horizontal lockup, the name's cap
  height is 0.46 of the symbol height and is centred on it; the gap is 64 units.

## Grounds

- Light grounds: colour files. Dark grounds, including the app's `#0C0C0E` /
  `#131418` surfaces: reversed files.
- On photos, place the mark on a solid tile of Ink or Paper.
- Keep 4.5:1 contrast between the b and its ground.

## Do not

- Stretch, skew, rotate or outline the mark.
- Move the superscript, change its size or make it round.
- Set "Business Agent" in another font or weight, or change the lockup spacing.
- Add shadows, gradients, glows or sparkles.
- Put the colour version on gold or on busy backgrounds.

## Notes

- Built on a 256-unit grid: stem 40 wide, bowl a half-disc of radius 60 with a
  radius-21 counter, bowl top bar thinned to 38 to balance the verticals,
  superscript 40 × 40 with an 8-unit corner radius.
- Tested at 16, 24, 32, 64 px, one colour, reversed, blurred and mirrored, with
  the logo-design skill's audit and test sheet.
- No trademark search has been done. Run one before any public launch.
